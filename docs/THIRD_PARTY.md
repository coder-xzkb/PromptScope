# Third-party assets and models

- `web/assets/astronaut.png`: NASA photograph of astronaut Eileen Collins, distributed by scikit-image as its public-domain `astronaut` sample. Downloaded unchanged from [scikit-image v0.21.0](https://github.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/blob/v0.21.0/skimage/data/astronaut.png). [Dataset attribution](https://scikit-image.org/docs/0.21.x/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.astronaut). Used as a computer-vision research example; no endorsement is implied.
- `web/assets/Manrope.ttf`: Manrope variable font from [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope), SIL Open Font License 1.1. License included next to the font.
- Grounding DINO Tiny and Base: official [IDEA-Research](https://github.com/IDEA-Research/GroundingDINO) models, Apache 2.0 model cards on Hugging Face. Weights downloaded separately, never committed. Revisions in `promptscope/model_revisions.json`.
- `examples/sample-runs.json`: actual local Tiny inference outputs generated by `scripts/smoke_model.py`; these are examples, not human annotations or benchmark results.
- App icon and interface code: created for PromptScope, covered by the project MIT license.
